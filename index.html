01 / ABOUT

An interest in patterns.
A habit of asking why.

I’m Wenyu Huang (黄文瑀), also known as Wing. I’m a physics undergraduate at UC San Diego, expecting to graduate in 2029. My interests connect physical systems, mathematics, and machine learning.

I like turning ideas into models, testing them with code, and finding out where the explanation breaks down. This is a place for the questions I’m exploring and the things I’m building along the way.
